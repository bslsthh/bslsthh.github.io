About

I'm Bishal, and this is just my story, as I can tell it.

I grew up in Sainamaina, Nepal. Since then I've trekked mountains, failed at businesses, learned to fly under a paraglider, cycled crazy distances, and spent more time alone thinking than I'd like to admit.

One day this might be all that's left of me. So I'm writing it mostly for myself.

  • 1997Born in dad's military camp in Imphal, India.
  • 2001Moved to our home in Ghamaha Khola, Sainamaina. This is still home.
  • 2013Finished school at Prabhat School. Stepped out into the real world.
  • 2014Switched high schools - New Horizon, then Sungava where I actually finished. My parents bought a bike. Felt like I got wings. Started exploring places like crazy.
  • 2015Launched my first business - a customizable jeans store in my hometown. Failed within 6 months. Learned a lot.
  • 2016Joined a bachelor's program. Left after 2 days. First trek to Langtang. Started making money online.
  • 2017Trekked Jumla-Rara Lake, Manang, Tilicho Lake. Something opened up. Started exploring seriously.
  • 2018Made real money from home with just a laptop. First international trip - Thailand, for Affiliate World conference. Came back different.
  • 2019Trekked Mardi Himal. Bought my first mountain bike. Rode solo from the hills to the mountains to the terai: Kathmandu-Butwal, Pokhara-Manang-Butwal, Butwal-Kathmandu.
  • 2020Covid. Moved to Patan. More cycling. Lived alone for 3 years. Thinking, building an online business in affiliate marketing, failing, getting back again.
  • 2021Learned rock climbing, paragliding, slacklining. Got the idea to start my own outdoor brand, starting with an ultralight hammock.
  • 2022Maldives for travel. Dubai for Affiliate World Conference. Went Kalinchowk. Advanced rock climbing in Bimalnagar and Manang. Got first taste of highlining. Built and tested the first hammock sample. Rigged highline in Bethanchowk with Saman dai.
  • 2023Launched yachu.co. Started building Yachu with no experience. Moved to a new apartment in Lalitpur. Adopted Blackie. Went to Singapore. Started making tent and product samples.
  • 2024Traveled to India. Left my online business completely - gave everything to Yachu. Trekked Makalu Barun Valley for 14 days with my team.
  • 2025Three weeks in Bali for travel. Three weeks in China across multiple cities for business. Watched Rabindra Dhat Brave FC fight on stage. Trekked to Panchpokhari.

I am focused on creating business, jobs, products, systems, health and wealth.

I never worked for anyone, no experience of what it felt like to be led. It was hard at first to work with different people, but right now, I am leading 12 people. Still learning and testing different methods. This shaped most of my life decisions. I am just getting started and success seems far distant, but here I am, moving one step at a time. It's just my journal, nothing more.

I learned attention is the currency. I feel like a fool sometimes, not knowing and getting distracted, but I get back on track. Let's see how it goes.
